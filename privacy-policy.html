Privacy policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Modern Cleaning Concept LP ("FotoFinish," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information in connection with the FotoFinish mobile application and web platform (collectively, the "Service").

1. Your consent

By using the Service, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, but doing so may limit your ability to use certain features.

2. What is business contact information?

Business contact information includes details that identify you in a professional context: your work email, job title, company name, and business address. To the extent permitted by law, this includes publicly listed business contact details.

3. What information we collect

a. Information You Provide: name, email address, phone number, password, payment information (for paid plans), business details, photos uploaded through the app, and any correspondence with us.

b. Automatically Collected Information: device type, unique device ID, IP address, operating system, browser type, and usage data. Some of this is non-personal and treated accordingly.

c. Location Data: If your organization uses attendance features, the mobile app collects precise device location, including while the app is running in the background, in order to record when you arrive at and leave the sites assigned to you. This location information forms part of the attendance record visible to administrators at the organization you work for. We also use location to determine your general area and to support site-proximity features.

You can turn off location access at any time in your device settings. Attendance features will not work without it, and the organization you work for may require it as a condition of using the Service.

If you provide us personal information about another person (for example, when creating accounts for your team), you are responsible for being authorized to do so.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Service
  • Provide automated and AI-assisted analysis of photos and related data (see "Automated Processing and AI" below)
  • Process transactions
  • Send service-related communications
  • Respond to support requests
  • Develop new features
  • Protect our legal rights and prevent fraud

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your information. We share it only:

  • As required by law
  • To protect safety or investigate fraud
  • With trusted service providers who process information on our behalf, are bound by confidentiality, and have no independent right to use the information (for AI features, see "Automated Processing and AI" below)
  • In connection with a business transaction (merger or acquisition), with notice provided
  • With your explicit consent

5a. Service providers we use

We share information with a small number of providers who process it on our behalf, under contract and only on our instructions. They are bound by confidentiality and have no independent right to use your information.

  • Google (Firebase) — app infrastructure, push notifications, usage analytics, and crash diagnostics
  • Google Maps — address lookup and mapping when setting up sites
  • Intercom — customer support messaging and in-product help
  • Stripe — payment processing for paid plans
  • Twilio (SendGrid) — service emails such as reports, invitations, and password resets, and SMS verification codes where enabled
  • Amazon Web Services — hosting and data storage

Providers used for automated and AI-assisted features are described in "Automated Processing and AI" below.

6. Automated processing and AI

The Service may include automated, AI-assisted analysis of photos and related submission data, such as comparing submitted photos against reference standards, drafting evaluation feedback, and generating report summaries.

To provide these features, we may use a small number of specialized AI infrastructure providers. They act only on our instructions as service providers: they are bound by confidentiality, have no independent right to use your information, are prohibited from using it to train AI models, and may not retain it beyond what is needed to process each request.

We never use your identifiable content, including photos, to train AI or machine-learning models, and we do not permit our service providers to do so. Automated analysis processes your organization's data on its own; we do not combine it with other customers' data. Aggregated or anonymized data that does not identify you or your organization may be used to improve our services and produce industry insights (see "Non-Personal Information" below). Customers may separately opt in, by written agreement, to future AI training or data-partnership programs.

Questions about automated features: privacy@fotofinish.com.

7. Your opt-out rights

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app. To request data deletion or to opt out of marketing communications, email privacy@fotofinish.com.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed to comply with legal obligations. All platform data (logs, photos, reports) is permanently deleted within 3 months of subscription cancellation. This deletion extends to any copies held by our service providers.

9. International data transfers

Your data may be processed in Canada, the United States, or other countries. By using the Service, you consent to these transfers.

10. Data accuracy

Please keep your information up to date. Notify us of changes at privacy@fotofinish.com.

11. Non-personal information

Aggregated or anonymized data may be used for any business purpose, including improving our services and producing industry insights, and is not subject to this policy.

12. Third-party links

The Service may link to third-party sites or tools. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before engaging.

13. Children

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact privacy@fotofinish.com and we will delete it promptly.

14. Security

We use physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will notify you by posting the updated version here and, for material changes, by email. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance.

16. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at privacy@fotofinish.com.
