Training for admin users

Set up sites, configure FotoFlows, manage users, and read your results. Everything you need to run FotoFinish for your organization.

Master every part of the admin dashboard

Click each module to learn how to set up and manage your FotoFinish account.

Setting up your first site

Every location you want to monitor is added as a "site" in FotoFinish. Setting up a site takes under 5 minutes and only requires a name, location details, and reference photos.

  • Navigate to Sites in your dashboard and click "New Site"
  • Enter the site name and address
  • Upload reference photos for each area you want monitored
  • Assign users who will be submitting reports at this site

Creating a FotoFlow

A FotoFlow defines what photos get taken at a site and how. Each FotoFlow contains slots, and each slot holds a set of reference images. The app randomly selects one image per slot each shift.

  • Open a site and click "Create FotoFlow"
  • Add slots for each area of the site (restrooms, kitchen, lobby, etc.)
  • Upload 2-5 reference photos per slot from different angles
  • Save and activate the FotoFlow — your team is ready to go

Managing users

Add on-site staff, supervisors, and other admins to your FotoFinish account. You control who sees which sites and what they can do. There is no limit on the number of users in any plan.

  • Go to Users in the main menu and click "Invite New User"
  • Enter their name, email, and role (on-site user, supervisor, or admin)
  • Assign them to their specific sites
  • They receive an email invitation with instructions to download the app

Reading your results

The Results Dashboard shows you a complete picture of compliance across all your sites. See which sites are performing well, which need attention, and how scores trend over time.

  • Use the History of Reports view to see all submissions chronologically
  • Click any report to view individual photos and evaluation scores
  • Use the trend charts to identify patterns across shifts or sites
  • Issue a corrective action ticket directly from any flagged photo
